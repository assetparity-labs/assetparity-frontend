INTEGRATION NOTES

One asset. Two execution paths.

Classic payments and Stellar Asset Contract transfers use the same underlying issued asset for G-account trustlines. This suite checks positive transfers between distinct, non-issuer accounts. Issuer mint/burn, contract-address balances, liabilities and event normalization need separate suites.

Amounts are integer base units. A displayed unit has seven decimal places. Account-address format checks on imported data are not checksum or ownership verification.

RPC simulations do not charge a transaction fee. Included failed transactions can charge fees. AssetParity preserves these execution labels and excludes native XLM fees from issued-asset balance parity.

Campaigns are stored locally in SQLite. Exported evidence includes the original observations and comparison result. No signing key or wallet is required to review a campaign.
